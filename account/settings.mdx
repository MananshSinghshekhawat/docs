SUITEe lets you personalise your dashboard experience with language settings, visual themes, and profile management. Your preferences are saved and remembered across sessions.

## Language settings

SUITEe supports multiple languages. The platform is currently available in:

| Language | Code | Flag |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **English** | EN | 🇬🇧 |
| **German** | DE | 🇩🇪 |

### How to switch languages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the language selector">
    Look for the **language selector** on the login page or within the dashboard. It is typically displayed as a **flag icon** or **language abbreviation** (e.g., "EN" or "DE").
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click and choose your language">
    Click on the language selector and choose your preferred language from the dropdown.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Interface updates immediately">
    The entire interface — menus, labels, buttons, and notifications — will update to the selected language instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The language setting affects only the **dashboard interface**. Guest-facing content (such as check-in instructions) may be configured separately based on the guest's preferred language.
</Info>

## Dark mode / Light mode

The SUITEe dashboard supports both **dark** and **light** visual themes, so you can choose the look that's most comfortable for you.

| Theme | Best For |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Light Mode** | Bright environments, daytime use |
| **Dark Mode** | Low-light environments, night shifts, reduced eye strain |

### How to switch themes

1. Look for the **theme toggle** in the dashboard settings or header area
2. Click to switch between **Dark Mode** and **Light Mode**
3. The change takes effect immediately

<Tip>
  SUITEe also supports a **System** option that automatically matches your device's theme preference. If your computer is set to dark mode, SUITEe will follow suit.
</Tip>

## Profile management

Your SUITEe account profile contains your basic information and access settings.

### What you can manage

| Setting | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Display Name** | Your name as shown in the dashboard and to other team members |
| **Email Address** | The email used for login and notifications |
| **Password** | Change your password for security (see [Forgot Password](/account/forgot-password)) |
| **Notification Preferences** | Choose which alerts you receive (email, push, or both) |

### Changing your password

If you want to change your password (not because you forgot it, but as a routine security measure):

1. Go to your **Profile** or **Account Settings** page in the dashboard
2. Look for the **"Change Password"** option
3. Enter your **current password**
4. Enter your **new password** and confirm it
5. Click **"Save"** or **"Update"**

<Warning>
  After changing your password, you may be logged out and asked to sign in again with the new password. This is normal and expected. If an email reset link expires, the platform alerts you immediately:
</Warning>

## Session management

- **Session timeout** — If you're inactive for an extended period, SUITEe may log you out for security. Simply log in again.
- **Multiple sessions** — You can be logged in on multiple devices simultaneously (e.g., your computer and phone)
- **Logout** — Always use the **"Log Out"** button to securely end your session, especially on shared computers

<Tip>
  **Security best practice:** Always log out of SUITEe when using a shared or public computer. Never save your password in a shared browser.
</Tip>
