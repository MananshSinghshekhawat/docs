For added security, SUITEe may require you to verify your identity using a **One-Time Password (OTP)** after you enter your login credentials. This ensures that even if someone knows your password, they cannot access your account without access to your email or phone.

## What is an OTP?

An OTP (One-Time Password) is a **temporary security code** — usually 4 to 6 digits — sent to your registered email address or phone number. Each code:

- Is **unique** and can only be used once
- **Expires** after a few minutes
- Is **generated automatically** by the system

<Info>
  OTP verification is a standard security practice used by banks, airlines, and major platforms worldwide. It adds a second layer of protection beyond your password.
</Info>

## How OTP verification works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your login credentials">
    On the login page, enter your **email** and **password** as usual, then click **"Log In"**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="OTP prompt appears">
    Instead of going directly to the dashboard, you'll see an **OTP verification screen** with a field to enter the code.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/otp-verification-actual.png" alt="SUITEe live staging OTP verification screen and email session validation guard" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check your email or phone">
    SUITEe sends the OTP to your registered **email address** or **phone number**. Open your email or check your SMS messages and find the code.

    The message will look something like:
    > **Your SUITEe verification code is: 482916**
    > This code expires in 5 minutes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the OTP code">
    Type the code into the verification field on the screen. Make sure to enter it exactly as shown — no spaces.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click 'Verify'">
    Click the **"Verify"** button. If the code is correct and hasn't expired, you will be taken to the **Admin Dashboard**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What if the OTP expires?

OTP codes are time-sensitive and typically expire after **3–5 minutes**. If your code has expired:

1. Click the **"Resend Code"** button on the verification screen
2. A **new code** will be sent to your email or phone
3. Use the **new code** — the old one is no longer valid

<Warning>
  Do not share your OTP code with anyone. SUITEe team members will **never** ask you for your OTP. If someone asks for it, do not provide it and report the incident to your administrator.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting OTP issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't receive the OTP code">
    - Check your **spam/junk** email folder
    - Make sure the email address on your account is correct
    - Wait 1–2 minutes — sometimes delivery can be slightly delayed
    - Click **"Resend Code"** to request a new one
    - If using SMS, ensure your phone has signal
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The code I entered says 'invalid'">
    - Make sure you're entering the **most recent** code (if you clicked "Resend", use the newest one)
    - Check for typos — the code is usually 4–6 digits with no letters or spaces
    - If the code has **expired**, request a new one using "Resend Code"
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I keep getting locked out after multiple failed OTP attempts">
    After several failed attempts, the system may temporarily lock OTP verification for security. Wait **5–10 minutes** and try again, or contact your property administrator for assistance.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  **Pro tip:** Keep your registered email inbox or phone nearby when logging in. The OTP step takes only a few seconds once you have the code.
</Tip>
