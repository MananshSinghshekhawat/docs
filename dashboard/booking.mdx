The **Booking** page is SUITEe's built-in reservation and guest journey management module. Designed for properties that operate without a third-party Property Management System (PMS), it provides an all-in-one hub to create reservations, handle contactless digital check-ins, generate digital room keys, and automate smart room climate controls.

Navigate to this page at:

```
https://hotel.suite-e.com/booking
```

## The 4-step guest journey workflow

The Booking engine powers four interconnected stages of hospitality operations:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/booking-guest-journey-workflow.png" alt="Guest journey workflow diagram showing the 4 steps: Buchungen, Gaste-Registrierung, Zimmerzugang, and Smarte Zimmersteuerung, alongside mobile app and desktop booking calendar screens" />
</Frame>

The four stages guide a guest from initial booking all the way through their stay:

| Stage | Name | Key Function | Connected Module |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Buchungen** | Create and manage reservations | [Rooms](/dashboard/rooms) |
| **2** | **Gäste-Registrierung** | Contactless digital check-in | [Dashboard Overview](/dashboard/overview) |
| **3** | **Zimmerzugang** | Share PIN codes and mobile unlock | [Access Management](/dashboard/access-management) |
| **4** | **Smarte Zimmersteuerung** | Smart heating, AC, and lighting | [Energy Management](/dashboard/energy-management) |

---

### Step 1 — Bookings (Buchungen)

The first step covers reservation creation, calendar scheduling, and occupancy tracking:

- **Reservation Scheduling Matrix**: A comprehensive calendar view where every room configured in [Rooms](/dashboard/rooms) is mapped along horizontal rows, and booking dates span across columns.
- **Real-Time Booking Status Counters**:
  - **Alle Buchungen** (`170`): Total reservations recorded across the property.
  - **Gebucht** (`8`): Active upcoming bookings confirmed in the calendar.
  - **Eingecheckt** (`1`): Guests currently checked into their rooms.
  - **Ausgecheckt** (`1`): Guests who have completed check-out today.
  - **Storniert** (`160`): Cancelled bookings retained in the system for auditing and reporting.
- **Action Buttons**:
  - **`+ Neue Reservierung`**: Create a new reservation directly inside SUITEe without needing an external booking engine.
  - **`Synchronisieren`**: Synchronize calendar availability with connected booking channels (OTAs).
  - **View Toggles**: Switch between the multi-room timeline grid and a detailed list view.

---

### Step 2 — Guest registration (Gäste-Registrierung)

The second step replaces paper forms and front desk lines with a self-service mobile web portal:

- **Contactless Pre-Arrival Check-In**: Guests receive a secure check-in link prior to arrival.
- **Digital Guest Card (`Konto erstellen`)**:
  - Guest personal details (First name, Last name).
  - Verified mobile phone number and email address.
  - Check-in and check-out dates verification.
- **Automatic Identity Linking**: Once submitted, the guest profile is linked to their designated room and assigned smart lock credentials.

---

### Step 3 — Room access (Zimmerzugang)

The third step automates door access without requiring physical keys or keycards:

- **Digital Key Sharing**: Automatically delivers temporary access codes and smartphone unlock links directly to the guest upon verified check-in.
- **Mobile Door Unlock (`Entsperren`)**: Guests can tap a button on their smartphone web app to unlock their room door in real time.
- **Time-Restricted Security**: Door codes and digital keys are only valid during the exact duration of the reservation — activating at check-in time and immediately expiring at check-out time.
- **Access Audit Log**: Every unlock event is automatically transmitted to the [Activity Centre](/dashboard/activity-centre) for complete security tracking.

For hardware setup and door lock configurations, see [Access Management](/dashboard/access-management) and [Smart Locks](/devices/smart-locks).

---

### Step 4 — Smart room control (Smarte Zimmersteuerung)

The final step empowers guests to personalize their room environment while optimizing property energy costs:

- **In-Stay Guest Dashboard**: Guests access an intuitive in-room control panel on their smartphone (e.g. *Hallo, Art Kuchem*).
- **Climate Control (`Klimasteuerung`)**:
  - Real-time temperature display and interactive setpoint wheel (e.g. `20°C`).
  - Fan speed slider (`Lüftungsgeschwindigkeit`).
  - Mode selection: **Auto**, **Cool**, **Dry**, and **Fan**.
- **Energy Automation**: When a booking is checked out or marked vacant, room thermostats automatically revert to eco-mode to reduce utility costs.

To learn more about room energy automation, see [Energy Management](/dashboard/energy-management) and [Thermostats](/devices/thermostats).

## Interconnected dashboard modules

The Booking system connects with several other areas of the SUITEe platform:

- **[Dashboard Overview](/dashboard/overview)**: The main control hub. Clicking the **`×`** close button on the booking modal redirects here.
- **[Rooms](/dashboard/rooms)**: All rooms, room codes, and device assignments configured in the Rooms module appear as rows in the Booking schedule matrix.
- **[Activity Centre](/dashboard/activity-centre)**: Logs all reservation check-in events, smart lock unlocks, and digital key activations.
- **[Access Management](/dashboard/access-management)**: Manages door passwords, smart locks, and master PIN configurations linked to guest stays.
- **[Energy Management](/dashboard/energy-management)**: Handles temperature setback schedules and climate automation linked to reservation occupancy states.
