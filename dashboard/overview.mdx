The Admin Dashboard is the **first screen you see after logging in**. It is the central hub where property managers and staff monitor and control everything about their property — from guest check-ins to device health to energy consumption.

## What the dashboard shows you

The dashboard provides a **real-time summary** of your property's current status at a glance. Here's what each section displays:

| Dashboard Section | What It Shows | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Today's Check-ins** | List of guests arriving today with their room assignments | Know who's coming and ensure rooms are ready |
| **Today's Check-outs** | List of guests departing today | Track departures and notify housekeeping |
| **Room Status** | Visual overview of occupied, vacant, and in-cleaning rooms | See your property's availability at a glance |
| **Device Health** | Status of all smart devices (online/offline, battery levels) | Catch issues before they affect guests |
| **Recent Alerts** | Any issues needing attention (low battery, offline devices, failed access) | Respond quickly to operational problems |
| **Energy Overview** | Summary of energy consumption across rooms and current modes | Monitor energy savings in real-time |

<Info>
  The dashboard updates in **real-time**. As guests check in, devices change status, or alerts are triggered, the dashboard reflects these changes immediately — no manual refresh needed.
</Info>

## Alerts and notifications

The system keeps you informed about anything that needs attention through an alerts system.

### Types of alerts

| Alert Type | Examples |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Device Alerts** | Lock has low battery, thermostat offline, sensor not responding |
| **Access Alerts** | Unusual door activity, failed access attempts |
| **System Alerts** | Connection issues, PMS sync problems |
| **Maintenance Alerts** | Device needs servicing, gateway disconnected |

### How alerts work

<Steps>
  <Step title="System monitors continuously">
    SUITEe continuously monitors all devices, connections, and integrations across your property.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alert appears on the dashboard">
    When something needs attention, an alert appears on the dashboard with details about the issue and its severity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notification sent (if configured)">
    Depending on your settings, you may also receive email or push notifications for critical alerts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take action and resolve">
    View the alert details, take the appropriate action, and mark the alert as resolved once the issue is fixed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Best practice:** Check the dashboard at the start of every shift to review any alerts that may have come in overnight. Address critical alerts (like low battery warnings) before they impact guest experience.
</Tip>

## Multi-property management

If you manage multiple properties, the dashboard supports seamless switching between them:

- Use the **property selector** (usually a dropdown at the top of the dashboard) to switch between different properties
- Each property has its own dashboard view, devices, and settings
- You **do not need to log out** to switch between properties

<Info>
  Multi-property management is ideal for businesses that operate several hotels, apartment buildings, or rental properties from a single team. One login gives you visibility across all locations.
</Info>
