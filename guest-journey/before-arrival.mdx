The guest journey begins **before the guest even arrives** at your property. SUITEe automates the entire pre-arrival process so that when the guest walks through the door, everything is ready.

## Step-by-step: Before arrival

<Steps>
  <Step title="Guest makes a booking">
    The guest books through any channel:
    - Your property's **website**
    - **Booking.com**, **Airbnb**, **Expedia**, or any other OTA (Online Travel Agency)
    - **Phone** or **walk-in** booking entered by staff
    - Any other booking channel

    The booking is recorded in your **PMS** (Property Management System) — the central system that manages all your reservations.

    <Info>
      It doesn't matter which channel the booking comes from. As long as it ends up in your PMS, SUITEe will receive it automatically.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="PMS sends booking data to SUITEe">
    Your PMS automatically syncs the booking details to SUITEe. The data transferred includes:

    | Data Field | Example |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | **Guest name** | "Maria Schmidt" |
    | **Email / Phone** | "maria@example.com" / "+43 660 1234567" |
    | **Room assignment** | Room 204 |
    | **Check-in date & time** | September 15, 2026 at 15:00 |
    | **Check-out date & time** | September 18, 2026 at 11:00 |
    | **Reservation status** | Confirmed |

    <Tip>
      This sync happens **automatically** — no one needs to manually enter data into SUITEe. The PMS integration handles it all in the background.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="SUITEe prepares for the guest">
    Once the booking is received, SUITEe automatically:

    - **Generates a unique PIN code** or **mobile key** for the guest
    - **Schedules room pre-conditioning** — the thermostat will start preparing the room temperature before the guest's arrival
    - **Prepares check-in instructions** with access details, property information, and any special notes
  </Step>

  <Step title="Guest receives check-in instructions">
    Before arrival, the guest receives an **email or SMS** with:

    - **How to access the property** (building entrance instructions)
    - **Their access code** (PIN code) or link to the Guest Web App (for mobile key)
    - **Online registration link** — the guest can complete registration before arrival (like airline check-in)
    - **Property information** — address, parking, Wi-Fi details, etc.

    <Info>
      By completing registration online before arrival, the guest saves time. When they arrive, they can go straight to their room — no check-in desk needed.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Room preparation begins">
    As the check-in time approaches:
    - The **thermostat** begins pre-heating or pre-cooling the room to a comfortable temperature
    - By the time the guest arrives, the room is at the **perfect temperature**
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## What the guest sees

The guest's experience before arrival is simple and streamlined:

1. **They book** through their preferred channel (business as usual for them)
2. **They receive an email/SMS** with clear check-in instructions
3. **They can register online** before arrival (optional but recommended)
4. **They know exactly how to access** the property and their room before they arrive

No surprises, no confusion, no need to coordinate with reception.

---

## What staff sees in the dashboard

During the pre-arrival period, staff can see:

| Dashboard Section | What's Shown |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Upcoming Reservations** | The guest's booking with all details |
| **Access Status** | Whether access credentials have been generated (should show "Pending") |
| **Room Preparation** | Whether the room's thermostat is scheduled for pre-conditioning |

<Tip>
  **Best practice for staff:** Check the "Upcoming Reservations" view at the start of each day to see who's arriving. Verify that access codes have been generated and that rooms are ready.
</Tip>
