This page covers the guest journey from the moment they arrive at your property through their stay and departure. Everything described here happens with **minimal or zero staff involvement** — that's the power of SUITEe.

---

## Arrival and check-in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Guest arrives at the property">
    The guest arrives at your property. There's **no need to go to a reception desk** — they already have their access credentials from the pre-arrival instructions.

    If they haven't already, they can open the **Guest Web App** on their smartphone:
    - Click the link from the email/SMS they received
    - The app loads directly in the phone's browser — **no download needed**
    - Complete any remaining registration steps
  </Step>

  <Step title="Guest enters the building">
    At the **building entrance**, the guest uses their:
    - **Mobile key** — tap "Unlock" in the Guest Web App, or
    - **PIN code** — enter the code on the lock's keypad

    The door unlocks automatically. The guest proceeds inside.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guest-web-app.png" alt="SUITEe Guest Web App interface with Tap to Unlock door key, PIN code, and room climate controls" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Guest enters their room">
    At their **room door**, the guest uses the same method (mobile key or PIN) to unlock the door.

    When they walk in:
    - The room is already at a **comfortable temperature** (pre-heated/pre-cooled)
    - Lights may be on in a welcoming configuration
    - The guest is now **officially checked in** — no staff interaction needed

    <Info>
      The entire check-in process — from arriving at the property to being inside their room — typically takes **under 2 minutes**.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## During the stay

### What the guest can do

Once checked in, the guest has full control over their room experience through the **Guest Web App**:

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Temperature control** | Adjust room heating/cooling from their phone |
| **Door access** | Lock and unlock their room door from their phone |
| **Amenity access** | Access gym, spa, pool, or other amenities (if included in their booking) |
| **Local recommendations** | Discover nearby restaurants, attractions, and services (if configured) |
| **24/7 assistance** | Access help information and contact details anytime |

### Smart automations during the stay

SUITEe's automations work silently in the background to enhance the guest experience and save energy:

| Scenario | What Happens Automatically |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Guest leaves the room** | Occupancy sensor detects vacancy → heating/cooling reduces after a set time |
| **Guest returns** | Occupancy sensor detects presence → room returns to comfortable temperature |
| **Guest opens a window** | Window sensor detects it → heating/cooling pauses to avoid waste |
| **Guest closes the window** | Heating/cooling resumes normal operation |

### What staff can do during the stay

Staff monitor everything from the **Admin Dashboard**:

- **Real-time room status** — See which rooms are occupied, vacant, or being serviced
- **Device alerts** — Receive notifications about low batteries, offline devices, or unusual activity
- **Remote control** — Adjust room temperature or other settings remotely if needed
- **Guest support** — Generate new access codes, resend instructions, or respond to requests

## Check-out and departure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check-out time arrives">
    At the scheduled check-out time (e.g., 11:00 AM), SUITEe automatically:
    - **Expires the guest's mobile key and PIN code** — they can no longer open the room
    - The guest simply leaves — no need to "check out" at a desk or return any keys
  </Step>

  <Step title="Room switches to Eco Mode">
    Immediately after check-out:
    - The **thermostat** switches to **Eco Mode** (minimum energy usage)
    - **Lights** are turned off (if smart switches are installed)
    - The room enters energy-saving state until the next guest
  </Step>

  <Step title="Housekeeping is notified">
    The dashboard **automatically notifies housekeeping** that the room is now vacant and ready to be cleaned. Staff can see which rooms need attention in the room status view.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Room resets for the next guest">
    Once cleaned, the room status is updated. When the next guest's reservation approaches:
    - New access credentials are generated automatically
    - Room pre-conditioning is scheduled
    - The cycle begins again seamlessly
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Guest Web App

The Guest Web App is the guest's companion throughout their stay. Here's a summary:

| Feature | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Access method** | Web browser — no app download required |
| **How guests get it** | Link sent via email/SMS before arrival |
| **Digital room key** | Unlock building and room doors from the phone |
| **Room controls** | Adjust temperature and lighting |
| **Registration** | Complete guest registration online |
| **Local info** | Nearby restaurants, attractions, and services |
| **Help** | Contact information and assistance resources |
| **Account creation** | Not required — the link is all they need |

<Warning>
  **For Help Centre staff:** If a guest says "I can't open my door," first verify:
  1. Are they using the Guest Web App or the correct PIN?
  2. Is the reservation active in the dashboard?
  3. Has access been generated? (Check the Access Status)
  4. Is the lock online? (Check device status)
  5. Does the lock have sufficient battery?

  See [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) for a complete diagnostic checklist.
</Warning>
